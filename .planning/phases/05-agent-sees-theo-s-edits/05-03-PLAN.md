RESEARCH correction (record it in the SUMMARY): CONTEXT says every board_read and board_edit by the agent marks the board seen. This plan marks seen on board_read and on a stale or not-found reply, because those results carry the whole board. An applied board_edit result carries only the agent's own items, so it marks seen only through the conditional advance built in plan 05-01. A delegate never marks seen.

Output: the seen marks in board_tools.py, their tests, one shared snippet helper, the 30-turn snippet and its test.
</objective>
